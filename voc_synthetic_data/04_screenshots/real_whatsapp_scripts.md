# Real WhatsApp screenshot scripts (5)

Goal: about 5 real screenshots of messy, real-world layouts to test OCR beyond the clean HTML renders.

## How to capture
- Use two WhatsApp numbers (or you + a friend). **You** send the *customer* lines, the other person sends the *support* lines.
- Type the lines exactly as written. Do not fix Hinglish spelling.
- Vary the conditions on purpose: dark mode for 2 of them, one with a wallpaper, one scrolled mid-chat, one slightly cropped, one at low brightness.
- Take a normal phone screenshot. Save as `real_01.png` ... `real_05.png` into `04_screenshots/real/`.
- Change the contact name to `FitApp Support` and blur nothing: all numbers and names here are fake.

## real_01  (en)

- **You (customer):** Hi, my payment failed in the app but Rs 2,999 was debited from my bank account.
- **Other person (support):** Sorry about that. Is your premium active now?
- **You (customer):** No, premium is still not active. It has been 3 days.
- **Other person (support):** We will look into it and revert.
- **You (customer):** Please refund it if you cannot activate the plan.
